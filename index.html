Capacity and scheduling optimization

A healthy overall utilization rate was hiding a class-level imbalance. I combined enrollment, capacity, demand, and instructor availability to identify the constraint and support a workable schedule adjustment.

01 · CHALLENGE

Uneven class load

Enrollment and capacity were not balanced across active programs, leaving one class above its workable level.

02 · ANALYSIS

Find the real constraint

Compared class enrollment, available seats, Program B demand, and instructor availability.

03 · INTERVENTION

Adjust the schedule

Redistributed student load and aligned added coverage with both demand and teaching availability.

04 · IMPACT

Restore balance

Reduced overcapacity while creating a repeatable weekly view of class-level utilization.

How the work was done

Objective

Identify the class-level imbalance and recommend a workable schedule change without ignoring demand or instructor constraints.

Data / inputs

My role

Analysis

Recommendation

How the work was done

Objective

Reduce repeated clarification and move complete inquiry information to the director faster.

Data / inputs

Inquiry details, required consultation fields, follow-up status, and observed processing time before and after the redesign.

My role

Mapped the handoffs, defined required fields, built the form and tracker, automated response capture, and added status controls.

Analysis

Traced delays to incomplete and inconsistent intake, manual re-entry, and limited visibility into pending cases.

Recommendation

Capture required information once, standardize categories, and use a centralized queue for review and follow-up.

Implementation

Google Form submissions populated Google Sheets automatically, with status and summary fields supporting director review.

Deliverable

Standardized intake form, automatically populated inquiry tracker, and director-ready review summary.

Stakeholder / decision

I designed and implemented the workflow; the director used the structured output for consultation and follow-up decisions.

Tools / methods

Google Forms · Google Sheets · automated data capture · workflow mapping · status tracking

Measurement note: The 40% figure is the observed reduction in consultation processing time after the redesigned workflow was introduced. Identifying records are not displayed.

Project 03
Market Intelligence & Decision Support

Independent school market intelligence and outreach prioritization

The raw school data contained duplicates, inconsistent formats, missing fields, and irrelevant records. I cleaned and enriched the dataset, then segmented 325 validated schools to identify 42 priority targets for international-student outreach and director review.

01 · AUDIT

Assess data quality

Reviewed 412 raw records for duplicates, missing fields, inconsistent formats, incorrect values, and irrelevant entries.

02 · CLEAN

Standardize and validate

Removed 87 duplicate or invalid records and standardized school names, locations, and multi-source formats.

03 · ENRICH

Complete and segment

Resolved missing values and added distance, religious affiliation, and outreach-relevant school attributes.

04 · PRIORITIZE

Create the target view

Compared the validated school universe and delivered 42 priority outreach targets to the director.

How the work was done

Objective

Create a reliable target base and identify independent schools with stronger fit for international-student outreach.

Data / inputs

412 records from BC government data and additional public research, with duplicates, missing fields, inconsistent formats, and invalid rows.

My role

Audited, deduplicated, standardized, validated, enriched, segmented, and prioritized the school records.

Analysis

Isolated independent schools, standardized location and affiliation fields, completed required values, then compared distance, religion, and outreach relevance.

Recommendation

Prioritize 42 schools whose validated attributes made them more relevant for the intended international-student outreach.

Implementation

The cleaned tracker and prioritized target view were handed to the director for outreach review and action.

Deliverable

Cleaned 325-school dataset, standardized research tracker, and prioritized list of 42 outreach targets.

Stakeholder / decision

I owned data preparation and target recommendation; the director reviewed which schools to move forward for outreach.

Tools / methods

Google Sheets · data cleaning · validation · missing-value handling · enrichment · segmentation

Data-quality note: 87 duplicate or invalid records were removed. Required-field completeness increased from 68% to 96%, enabling consistent comparison across the 325 validated profiles.

From raw records to an outreach decision

The value came from improving data quality first, then applying consistent criteria to narrow the validated school universe into an actionable target list.

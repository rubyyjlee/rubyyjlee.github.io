01 / Capacity & Resource Allocation

Class Capacity & Utilization Optimization

Enrollment looked manageable at the overall program level, but class-level data showed an uneven distribution of students. I structured enrollment, capacity, and instructor availability data to identify the bottleneck and support scheduling changes.

Problem
Aggregate enrollment numbers did not clearly show where capacity pressure was occurring. One program had become overcapacity while available seats remained elsewhere, making scheduling increasingly reactive.
Analysis
I compared enrollment, class capacity, utilization rate, instructor availability, and schedule constraints across active programs.
The overall utilization rate looked healthy, but the average masked a concentration problem at the class level.
Decision
Based on demand and instructor availability, I supported schedule adjustments and redistributed students to reduce pressure on the overcapacity class while maintaining available instructional coverage.
Result
Overcapacity was reduced by 33%. The tracking structure also created a clearer view of class-level utilization for future scheduling decisions.
02 / Workflow & Decision Support

Inquiry Workflow & Reporting Improvement

Customer inquiry information was arriving through multiple channels and required repeated manual review before consultations. I redesigned the intake process so information could be reviewed in a structured format and tracked through follow-up.

Problem
Inquiry details were inconsistent and often required additional back-and-forth before the program director could review the case and determine the next step.
Approach
I created a structured intake form connected to a centralized spreadsheet, standardizing the information collected and making inquiry status and follow-up needs easier to review.
Operational Impact
The new workflow reduced repeated information gathering and allowed the director to review key details before consultation.
The goal was not simply to collect more information, but to make the information immediately usable for the next decision.
Result
Consultation processing time decreased by 40%. The process also created a clearer record of inquiries, outstanding follow-ups, and consultation status.

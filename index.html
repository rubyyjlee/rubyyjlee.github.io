Project 01
Operations Analytics

Capacity and scheduling optimization

A healthy overall utilization rate was hiding a class-level imbalance. I combined enrollment, capacity, demand, and instructor availability to identify the constraint and support a workable schedule adjustment.

01 · CHALLENGE

Uneven class load

Enrollment and capacity were not balanced across active programs, leaving one class above its workable level.

02 · ANALYSIS

Find the real constraint

Compared class enrollment, available seats, Program B demand, and instructor availability.

03 · INTERVENTION

Adjust the schedule

Redistributed student load and aligned added coverage with both demand and teaching availability.

04 · IMPACT

Restore balance

Reduced overcapacity while creating a repeatable weekly view of class-level utilization.

Project 03
Research & Decision Support

School research and outreach intelligence

Public school information existed across multiple sources, but it was not ready for consistent comparison or outreach planning. I turned fragmented information into a structured research base covering 325 independent schools across Metro Vancouver.

01 · CHALLENGE

Scattered information

Public data, school websites, and individual research notes did not provide one consistent view for comparison.

02 · RESEARCH

Build the school universe

Used BC public education data to identify relevant independent schools, then added school-level research.

03 · STRUCTURE

Standardize the fields

Organized school type, location, relevant attributes, and research status into a centralized tracker.

04 · USE

Support outreach decisions

Created a consistent basis for target identification and short-term schooling outreach preparation.

Operational improvement, not dashboard decoration.

My approach starts with the operating question: what is going wrong, what decision is blocked, and what information would make that decision easier?

I then define the baseline, identify the constraint, make the smallest practical intervention, measure the effect, and leave behind a monitoring view that supports the next decision.

Data note: These case studies are based on real operational improvement work. Names and identifying details have been removed, and dashboard values have been anonymized or reconstructed to protect confidential information.
